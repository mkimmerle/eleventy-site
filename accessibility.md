    <h2 class="u-h2">Beyond the Automated Scan</h2>
    <p class="u-b1">
        Automated accessibility scanners are good at catching surface-level issues, but they don’t tell you whether a site is actually usable. I combine site-wide automated scanning with targeted manual testing — including keyboard navigation, screen reader checks, and review of common template-level failures.
    </p>
    <p class="u-b1">
        The focus isn’t just on individual errors, but on identifying the patterns in your templates and components that repeat across the site. Once those patterns are fixed, most accessibility issues disappear across the entire codebase.
    </p>
